# Opening decisions and hook text

## Choose the opening from the actual recording

Read/listen for a specific result, an unexpected but supported contrast, a clear problem or a useful action. Note two plausible source ranges only when there is a meaningful choice. Test the selected line followed immediately by the intended next line. A pronoun without its subject, a claim without its qualifier or a cliffhanger never answered by the body is a failed opening even if the first sentence sounds punchy.

Example: “You don't need to train to failure on every set” cannot become “Train to failure on every set.” A hesitation inside a qualifier is less important to remove than preserving the sentence's meaning. “Your two-hour workout down to forty minutes — here's how” is a promise; keep the explanatory context rather than using the number as a universal fitness guarantee.

Default to moving the chosen range; repeating it later is intentional only when the second occurrence develops it. Preserve needed transitions or request a short pickup if the source cannot join naturally. Show an actual cut, not just rewritten hook copy.

## Hook text is not the transcript rail

A hook overlay tells the viewer what to notice; subtitles help them follow what is being said. Avoid making both say the same long sentence. Start with one short main title. A second line can belong to the same title; add a subtitle only if it supplies genuinely necessary new information.

Position before styling: inspect the complete display window, find space above the facial features or another unoccupied region, and keep the person/evidence visible. For a plain overlay, preserve the source's full-screen framing; don't shrink the trainer into a corner just to fit extra words. An intentional PiP explanation is a separate layout decision.

Use a conservative starting envelope of around 15% side margins and 18–20% top clearance for vertical phone playback, then verify against the real player and subject. These are experience-derived presets, not official permanent platform rules. Shadows/backgrounds count toward the bounds. If it won't fit at a readable size, shorten or reflow it; do not solve every fit problem by shrinking the font.

Render type at the final canvas size. Inspect first frame, held state and exit. Do not obscure eyes, mouth or exercise form. Numbers, units and visual comparisons must agree with the spoken claim. Examples of package capabilities are examples, not a mandatory house style.
