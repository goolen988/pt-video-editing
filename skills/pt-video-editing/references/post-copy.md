# Posting copy

Write from the final rendered content. Offer a concise natural description, an appropriate action (save/use/comment/book if justified), and a small set of relevant searchable topic tags. Match the trainer's audience and voice. Keep uncertain health/fitness claims qualified; do not turn a general demonstration into a guaranteed outcome.

Descriptions and hashtags cannot compensate for unclear content. Historical finance-account rules and claims about algorithm penalties are not portable evidence. Verify a target platform's current limits if exact counts matter. Save copy beside the final candidate; do not post it automatically. Distinguish on-video captions (spoken words) from the post description.
